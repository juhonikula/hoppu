              How hoppu works

              hoppu finds public transport routes in the Helsinki region like the HSL app, but lets you plan transfers as tight as you dare and shows which ones you can still make walking or running.

              Searching

              • From / To: type a stop or an address and pick a suggestion. ☆ saves a place; saved places show as buttons under To. The crosshair in From uses your location.
              • Search plans with the Normal settings, at the time you set (empty = now; Leave at or Arrive by).
              • Hoppu! plans with the Hoppu settings, leaving now (empty From = your location), and opens the fastest route straight away. Back shows the other routes. Its routes start now: you walk to the first stop right away and wait there if you're early, instead of leaving at the last moment.
              • Earlier options and Show more options load routes before and after the ones shown.

              Reading a route

              Tap a result for its details: a map and a timeline. Each transfer shows how long you have between arriving and the next departure, coloured by your own walking and running speeds over the walk:

              • green 2 min or more to spare walking, amber under 2 min, red under 1 min;
              • violet only makeable running;
              • struck through: too short even running.

              The first boarding is judged the same way, counted from when you searched ("6:00 to the stop"): how long you have to get there from your starting point if you leave right away.

              The chip next to it shows the walk and run times; ⚠ marks walks of 400 m or more. "live" and "late" badges are realtime predictions. Vehicles on your route move on the map live, with an arrow for their heading.

              Transfer options

              Show options here under any boarding plans again from where the previous vehicle drops you, around the planned time (for the first one: your starting point, from when you searched). You see the two departures before the current one, the current one and two backups, also from other stops nearby, with the walk to each, the arrival and the difference to your plan. Original marks the departure of the route as first planned; once you follow another option, that one is current. Departures too short even running stay on the list, faded and struck through: a delay can still make them. To find two earlier ones, the search looks from 10 minutes before you arrive, then 20 and 30 if needed (a request each). Tap one (or its dashed line or live vehicle on the map) to follow it. Refresh options fetches fresh realtime. For a new plan from where you are now, search again from your location.

              My time saves how long a specific change took you (e.g. "Helsinki → Rautatientori: 2:10", typed m:ss), with Save walked or Save ran. Every save is kept: your average walking time there replaces the walk estimate for that change on every route, your average running time the run estimate (a mode you haven't timed is scaled from the other by your speeds). In Settings, under the walking and running speed, the times with a known distance give your average pace. It's only shown there: the planner keeps using the speeds you set.

              Stops of a vehicle

              Tap a line's badge in the timeline, the line or its live vehicle on the map, or the vehicle on a result card. A sheet lists its stops from where you board to the end of the line, and where the vehicle is now ("Coming" while it's still on its way to your stop).

              Show options at a stop shows what you'd catch by getting off there instead: walking the rest of the way, or the next vehicles with the time you'd have to change. Nothing changes until you tap an option.

              Going back

              Each option you follow makes a new version of the route. Chips under the map list them with their arrival times (Planned, #2, …), and earlier versions show on the map as grey lines where they differ. Tap a chip or a grey line to switch back.

              Settings

              Two profiles: Normal is used by Search, Hoppu by Hoppu!. Empty fields use the planner's default.

              Transfer margin (min)
              Extra time the planner keeps for every change. The HSL app uses 1.5 min; 0 plans as tight as the timetable allows.
              Speed unit
              How both profiles show and take the speeds below: km/h, m/s or pace in min/km (type 5:30, or 5.30 / 5,30 on a number pad). The grey line under each speed shows it in the other units.
              Walking speed
              Used for planning, and for the transfer colours.
              Running speed
              Only for the colours: transfers you can make only at this speed are violet.
              Walk reluctance
              How much the planner avoids walking. 1 = a minute of walking counts like a minute of riding; the HSL app uses 1.8.
              Board cost (s)
              Penalty per boarding. A small one (Hoppu: 90 s) avoids routes with extra changes that arrive at the same time.
              Transfer cost (s)
              Extra penalty per change (HSL app: 180 s).
              Results
              How many routes one search returns.
              Compare with HSL app settings
              Plans the same trip with the HSL app's settings too (one extra request) and tells how much earlier you arrive.

              Saved places and Your transfer times can be removed here. Forget API key removes your key from this browser.

              Your key and data

              Everything stays in this browser: your key, places, settings and times. hoppu has no server; it asks Digitransit directly with your key, about one request per search, per opened stop list and per "Show options" (up to four for a transfer's options). Digitransit's free keys are for personal use, a few hundred requests a day.

              On a phone, "Add to Home screen" installs hoppu as an app; it then opens without a network too (searching needs one).
